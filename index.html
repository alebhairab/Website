Prospective PhD Candidate in Solar Physics & Space Weather

I am a physicist from Nepal studying the Sun and its effect on near-Earth space. I am seeking a fully funded PhD position in solar physics, heliophysics, or space weather at a university in Europe or the United States.

I am completing an M.Sc. in Physics (Astrophysics major, Biomedical Physics minor) at the Central Department of Physics, Tribhuvan University. My thesis follows the evolution of butterfly-shaped coronal holes across the solar disk, and it is now published in BIBECHANA. I have 3 peer-reviewed journal articles and 2 magazine articles, and I work with satellite and ground-based data using wavelet analysis, cross-correlation, and statistical methods.
